  Wind500   |  1.528    |  1.289  |   0.239     |  No
  Wind10    |  1.542    |  1.434  |   0.108     |  No

FINDING: T-insertion reduces NRMSE for all downstream variables but does NOT
bring any of them below the NRMSE=1.0 skill threshold. The model remains
unskilled for all variables even with perfect T500 knowledge.

The largest benefit is to Z500 (0.602 reduction, from 1.962 to 1.360), consistent
with the hypsometric relationship linking layer-mean temperature to geopotential
thickness. U500 improves modestly via thermal-wind balance. Wind10 barely responds
(reduction of 0.108) — surface winds are driven by boundary-layer dynamics, not
by 500 hPa temperature alone.

Physical interpretation: T500 is a secondary variable in FCNv2's learned dynamics.
The model's forecast errors are driven primarily by incorrect winds and
geopotential, and correcting T500 alone cannot restore global skill.

4.  WIND INSERTION (WIND-INS) — NRMSE IMPACT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Wind-insertion forces U500+V500 = ERA5 truth from day 30 onward.
U500 and Wind500 NRMSE → 0.000 trivially.

Downstream NRMSE in mature phase:

  Variable  |  Control  |  Wind-Ins  |  Reduction  |  Below 1.0?
  ──────────┼───────────┼────────────┼─────────────┼────────────
  T500      |  1.717    |  0.595     |   1.122     |  YES ✓
  Z500      |  1.962    |  0.396     |   1.566     |  YES ✓
  Wind10    |  1.542    |  0.594     |   0.948     |  YES ✓

CRITICAL FINDING: Wind-insertion is the only experiment that brings multiple
variables below NRMSE=1.0, restoring genuine predictive skill beyond day 30.

Z500 NRMSE drops from 1.962 to 0.396 — a reduction of 1.566 NRMSE units, the
largest single improvement in the entire analysis. With correct winds, the
model's Z500 errors fall to just 40% of natural atmospheric variability. This
directly confirms that FCNv2's geopotential forecast errors at long lead times
are caused by incorrect wind dynamics, particularly the misrepresentation of
baroclinic growth in the storm-track region.

T500 NRMSE drops from 1.717 to 0.595 — again below 1.0. Temperature errors in
the model are primarily caused by incorrect horizontal advection (∂T/∂t ≈ -u·∇T):
with correct winds, the model accurately transports temperature across the grid.

Wind10 also falls below 1.0 (0.594), confirming that the model has learned a
strong 500 hPa → surface wind coupling consistent with the barotropic component
of midlatitude flow.

Physical interpretation: Wind (U+V) is the dominant prognostic variable in
FCNv2. The horizontal circulation controls both the mass field (via divergence and
vorticity) and the temperature field (via advection). Incorrect winds are the
primary cause of all long-lead forecast degradation.

  Wind500   |  1.528    |  0.576    |   0.952     |  YES ✓
  Wind10    |  1.542    |  0.714    |   0.828     |  YES ✓

FINDING: Geo-insertion brings all four downstream variables below NRMSE=1.0,
making it the most broadly effective experiment.

T500 NRMSE reaches its lowest value of the entire analysis at 0.381 (geo-ins),
even lower than the 0.595 from wind-ins. Correct Z500 encodes layer-mean
temperature through hypsometric balance almost perfectly:
  T̄ = (g/R) · ΔZ / ln(p₁/p₂)

U500 NRMSE drops to 0.516 via geostrophic wind recovery:
  u_g = -(g/f) · ∂Z/∂y

Wind10 reaches 0.714 NRMSE — the highest Wind10 NRMSE of any skillful experiment,
confirming that geopotential is a less efficient driver of surface winds than
direct 500 hPa wind correction.

Physical interpretation: Z500 is the richest single variable in FCNv2's
state vector — it encodes the large-scale flow (geostrophic wind), the thermal
structure (hypsometric), and indirectly the surface circulation. Correcting Z500
globally restores skill for the entire atmospheric column.

6.3  The Wind-Ins vs Geo-Ins asymmetry:

  Wind-Ins → Z500 = 0.396  |  Geo-Ins → U500 = 0.516

  Wind-insertion fixes Z500 better than geo-insertion fixes U500. This asymmetry
  reveals the causal direction of FCNv2's dynamics:
  → Errors propagate more strongly from Wind → Z than from Z → Wind.
  The wind field is the primary driver; geopotential follows.

7.  KEY SCIENTIFIC FINDINGS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Finding 1 — Control run loses all skill by day 30:
  All 5 variables exceed NRMSE=1.0 in the mature phase. FCNv2 without correction
  is no better than climatological persistence at lead times > 30 days.

Finding 2 — Wind is the dominant prognostic variable:
  Wind-insertion achieves the largest absolute NRMSE reduction for Z500
  (1.566 units, from 1.962 to 0.396), confirming that horizontal wind dynamics
  govern the evolution of the large-scale circulation in FCNv2.

Finding 3 — Z500 is the richest information carrier:
  Geo-insertion achieves the lowest NRMSE for T500 (0.381), U500 (0.516),
  and Wind500 (0.576) — better than any other experiment for these variables.
  A single scalar field (Z500) encodes thermal, wind, and mass structure.

Finding 4 — T-insertion is insufficient to restore skill:
  Despite perfectly correcting T500, all downstream NRMSE remain above 1.0.
  This shows that FCNv2's thermal state is a consequence of its dynamical state,
  not a driver of it — temperature errors are symptoms, not causes.

Finding 5 — Wind10 is the hardest variable to fix:
  Even with perfect 500 hPa wind or geopotential insertion, Wind10 NRMSE
  remains at 0.594–0.714. Near-surface winds couple weakly to the free
  troposphere in this model, possibly due to missing boundary-layer physics.
